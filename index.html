Our Story

How two worlds became one

How We Met

It was August 2021. We found each other at the Everson Museum pre-prep program — then again at Slocum Hall, then the architecture computer lab. Shared experiences became shared silences that didn’t need filling. We didn’t know it yet, but we’d already begun.

The First Date

That summer, the city became ours — subway rides with no destination, dinners that lasted past closing, and a feeling neither of us could name. On July 19, 2022, Tony finally said what we both already knew. No more “just friends.” Just us.

Falling in Love

It wasn’t one grand moment — it was a thousand small ones. Study sessions at Whitman that turned into dreams shared out loud. Learning each other’s worlds. Realizing that the person sitting across from you is the one you want across from you forever.

The Proposal

On April 9, 2026, Tony led Priyal to a private recital space in Issaquah, Washington — and played. A piano. A concert. Just for her. By the last note, there were tears and laughter and a question that only ever had one answer. Yes. Always yes.

April 17, 2027

From a computer lab in Syracuse to a celebration in Richmond — this is where our story brings us next. Two hearts, two cultures, one forever. We can’t wait to share it with you.
